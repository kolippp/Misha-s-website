BIOGRAPHY.

THE PERSON BEHIND THE PERFORMANCE

Misha Mysyshyn is a dynamic and charismatic circus artist specializing in Rolla Bolla and juggling. Originally from Ukraine, he has been performing since the age of eight, developing a distinctive style that combines impressive balance skills, technical precision, playful energy, and a natural connection with the audience.

His artistic journey began in Ukraine, where he studied at the Kyiv Municipal Academy of Circus and Variety Arts before continuing his professional training at the Berlin State Artist School in Germany. Through years of dedicated practice, Misha has developed a versatile act that blends high-level technical difficulty with humor, charm, and engaging stage presence.

His signature Rolla Bolla performance pushes the limits of balance and coordination, combining multiple rolling cylinders, dynamic movements, and unexpected tricks. Alongside his balancing skills, Misha incorporates juggling with balls and clubs, creating a visually engaging performance that builds in intensity and keeps audiences captivated from beginning to end.

Misha has performed on prestigious international stages, including Wintergarten Varieté in Berlin and Tigerpalast Varieté Theater in Frankfurt. His international experience also includes a European tour with Eastfire and the US tour of Cirque Dreams Holidaze, a production presented by Cirque Dreams, part of the Cirque du Soleil entertainment group.

In 2026, Misha received a Special Prize at the GauklerFESTung in Koblenz, recognizing his talent and artistic presence.

Driven by a passion for variety entertainment and a constant desire to push his limits, Misha brings a combination of athleticism, personality, and showmanship to every performance. Whether performing on an intimate stage or in a grand variety theater, his goal remains the same: to surprise the audience, create memorable moments, and make extraordinary skills feel effortless.

Energetic, charismatic, and always pushing the limits of balance - Misha Mysyshyn brings his own distinctive style to the stage.
